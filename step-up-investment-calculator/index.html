Why Step-Up SIP is the Ultimate Wealth Multiplier

Most individuals start investing with an amount affordable early in their careers. However, as salary and income rise with promotions and annual increments, maintaining a flat SIP leaves massive surplus uninvested.

A Step-Up SIP (or Top-Up SIP) commits to raising your monthly contribution by a modest percentage (e.g., 10%) once every year. Over 15 to 20 years, a 10% annual step-up can often double or triple your final retirement corpus compared to a static SIP.

Aligns With Salary Increments

If you receive an 8-10% annual salary hike, channeling a 10% increase to your investments preserves your savings rate.

Huge Terminal Corpus Boost

A flat 20,000/month at 12% for 20 years yields ~2.0M. Stepping up 10% each year yields nearly ~4.8M!

Counteracts Lifestyle Inflation

Stepping up contributions keeps lifestyle creep in check while accelerating financial freedom.

Step-Up Annual Contribution Escalation

Monthly contribution in Year y increments by annual step-up rate s%: 

Monthly Deposit in Year y = Starting Monthly × (1 + s)^(y - 1)

Example:
• Year 1: 20,000 / month
• Year 2 (+10%): 22,000 / month
• Year 3 (+10%): 24,200 / month
• Year 4 (+10%): 26,620 / month
• Year 5 (+10%): 29,282 / month

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I step up my investment each year?▼

A step-up rate between 5% and 10% is standard and easily manageable for most salaried professionals who receive annual compensation reviews.

Can I stop stepping up after reaching a ceiling?▼

Yes, in practice you can cap your monthly contributions once your income stabilizes or other family milestones arise.
