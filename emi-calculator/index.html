How Reducing-Balance Loan EMI Works

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender on a specified date each month. Each EMI consists of both a principal component and an interest component.

In a reducing-balance loan, interest is calculated each month strictly on the outstanding balance. As principal is repaid over time, the interest portion of your EMI decreases, and the principal portion increases.

The Universal Reducing-Balance EMI Formula

The standard mathematical formula for calculating monthly installment: 

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

Where:
• P = Loan Principal Amount
• r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
• n = Total Number of Monthly Installments (Tenure in Years × 12)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is reducing-balance EMI vs flat-rate EMI?

Reducing-balance EMI calculates interest only on the unpaid loan balance, which is significantly cheaper than flat-rate interest where interest is calculated on the original loan amount for the entire tenure.

How does interest rate affect my total loan cost?

Even a 0.5% reduction in interest rate on a 20-year loan saves massive amounts in total interest payments over time.
